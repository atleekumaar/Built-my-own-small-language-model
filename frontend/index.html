About SimpleWordTokenizer

This model uses whitespace-based word tokenization (re.split(" +", text)) rather than subword BPE. Attached punctuation marks (such as commas or periods) remain part of the word token (e.g. "beads." is a distinct token from "beads"). Any word or punctuation combination that never appeared in the 232 Africa Galore paragraphs maps directly to the special catch-all <UNK> token (Index 5,261).

Phase 5 Scientific Validation & Fair Model Comparison

Rigorous evaluation on identical 47 held-out validation paragraphs (15,916 UTF-8 bytes) with byte-normalized likelihood (BPB)

Scientific Methodology Warning — Cross-Tokenizer Perplexity Comparability: Per-token perplexity (PPL = exp(CE)) cannot be compared directly between Word and BPE models because their token granularities differ (6.3 bytes/token for Word vs. 3.5 bytes/token for BPE). Furthermore, the Word model's raw loss naively maps 22.7% of held-out tokens to <UNK> without modeling the unseen characters. To establish a fair benchmark, we evaluate Bits Per UTF-8 Byte (BPB) on identical text, both raw and adjusted for open-vocabulary unseen word entropy.
